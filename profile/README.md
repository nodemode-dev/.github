FormAI, free open-source desktop apps from NodeAgentc, Inc. Also, check out [NodeMode](https://www.nodemode.ai), our cloud-hosted enterprise platform.

Each Form app does one job well and runs on your own computer: your files stay on your machine. Many of them can also be driven by an AI agent through MCP when you want that, and they work fine without it.

## Get the apps

Download from **[formai.studio](https://formai.studio/apps/)**. Every release is built and signed by [FormCompiler](https://formai.studio/formcompiler/), our public build pipeline. FormPortal installs the apps and keeps them up to date.

## Help and contributing

- **Bugs and ideas:** open an issue in the app's repository.
- **Security problems:** report them privately from the repository's Security tab, never in a public issue. Each repository's SECURITY.md says what to expect.
- **Code:** read the repository's CONTRIBUTING.md first.
- **Anything else:** support@formai.studio for FormAI or support@nodemode.ai for NodeMode
